# Privacy

Payroll Prep for Small Businesses collects nothing.

- It runs entirely on files you give it, in your own workspace. It makes no network
  requests, sends no telemetry and has no accounts, servers or analytics.
- It needs names, employee IDs, pay rates, hours and sales. It never asks for Social
  Security numbers, bank details or dates of birth; if an export you give it contains
  them, leave those columns out.
- The files it writes (review workbook, run summary, upload files, history) stay in your
  workspace until you delete them or choose to send them somewhere.
- When you use it inside Claude, your conversation and the files you share are handled
  under your Claude account's own terms and privacy settings.
